Rene is a builder, connector and strategist. Over the past decade, she's lived in 7 cities and worked across 3 continents in roles spanning international finance, technology and industrial innovation. One thread has remained constant: working closely with founders and CEOs to translate ambitious ideas into strategy, communication and execution. Most recently she helped a 30-year-old industrial technology company expand into new global markets. Today, she brings that experience and a deep interest in storytelling, communication and systems-building to Eklipse, where she works with innovators to turn vision into scalable frameworks for growth.

Trajectory

Rene is interested in the trajectory of ambitious people, ideas and companies — and what allows them to become more fully what they are capable of being. Her edge is meaning-making: connecting events into narratives, finding patterns across people, markets and systems, and translating complexity into a clearer sense of what matters and where to go next. These days, that instinct is at the heart of her work with founders: turning moments of uncertainty into forward momentum. She believes the best kind of ambition expands what is possible without requiring us to trade humanity for progress, or people and planet for profit. Rene is always interested in hearing from founders and what becomes possible when direction and ambition align.
