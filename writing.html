Ideas on how people, capital and technology move — and what moves with them.

If you only read one

Each issue explores a moment of movement — where a founder, company, technology or market is today and where it may be going next. Through the Eklipse lens, In Transit examines the strategic work that happens in between: shifts in identity, narrative, and systems.

◇ My Story

Global. Strategic. Ambitious.

Rene is a builder, connector and strategist. Over the past decade, she's lived in seven cities and worked across three continents — at the UN, the Economist Intelligence Unit, and a run of startups and scale-ups — in roles spanning international finance, technology and industrial innovation. Her focus sits at the intersection of China, EV, geopolitics and ESG: macro research on China's economy, direct management access to major Chinese EV brands, and ESG due diligence using frameworks like SASB, CDP and GRI.

One thread has stayed constant: working side by side with founders and CEOs to turn big ideas into strategy, story and execution. Most recently, she helped a 30-year-old industrial technology company expand into new global markets.

At Eklipse, she brings that experience together with a love of storytelling and systems-building — helping innovators turn vision into frameworks built to scale.
